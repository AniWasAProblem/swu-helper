# Wicket Ewok Swarm (v18): analysis and pilot guide

## Analysis

### 1. Game-stage plan

- **Early (turns 1–3):**
  - **Turn-1 plays:** 30 units cost 2 or less, so you almost always have one (99.9% of opening hands). A 1-cost unit is in your opening seven 81% of the time.
  - **Turn 2:** a 2-drop plus a 1-drop. Ewok Brigade or Champions of Endor are the usual bodies.
  - **Turn 3:** Wicket deploys, once you reach 4 resources.
  - **Space:** Outland Protector, Y-Wing and V-19 give you a space unit from turn 1–2. Against space decks, having one early matters (see "Weaknesses").
- **Mid (turns 4–6):** you play two or three cards a turn, and every unit attacks with one extra power from Verdant Fortress.
  - **Ty Yorrick on turn 4:** with a resource every turn you reach 5 resources on turn 4. Ty is the turn-4 play when you hold him. Until then, the 2-drops and 3-drops carry the turn.
  - **Clear blockers:** Heavy Ion Cannon, Logray, Ewok Archers' Ambush, Reckless Sacrifice, Turning the Tide and Nightfall.
  - **Kill a big ship:** Dorsal Turret on a ready Y-Wing, V-19 or Outland Protector. The Cannon finishes a ship that has already been damaged.
  - **Kill a big ground unit:** Reckless Sacrifice deals 5 (6 with Ty Yorrick in play). Relentless Hunters attacks for 5 (6 with Verdant Fortress's Raid). Nightfall's +2 attack or the Cannon finishes the job. Each of these kills a 5-HP unit. A 6–7 HP unit needs two of them, or Han Solo's +3 on a ready attacker (see "Card-specific lines").
  - **Chip the base:** Cloud-Rider and Y-Wing, every round. Adamant Ewoks deals 1 to the base when it is played (2 with Ty Yorrick).
  - **Survive pings:** Ewok Brigade (4 HP), Archers, Logray and Chirpa (5 HP), and Cloud-Rider (4 HP) all survive a 1–3 damage ping. Logray turns each ping on a cheap friendly unit into one of yours.
- **Late (turn 7+):**
  - **Finishers:** Nightfall (+2 power and an extra attack), Han Solo (+3 power on one attack), Relentless Hunters, Ty Yorrick, and the Saboteurs for getting past Sentinels.
  - **Spare cards:** the Cannon turns cards you can no longer use (a stranded Turret, a late 1-drop) into 2 damage each round (3 with Ty Yorrick).
  - **Risk:** there are no big units, and your hand is usually only 2–4 cards by now. If the board is even on turns 8–9, the opponent's cards are better than yours. This deck has to win before turn 9.

### 2. Cost curve

11 / 25 / 10 / 3 / 1 cards at costs 1–5, average 2.16. That is a low, dense aggro curve. The one 5-drop is a late-game threat, not a reason to ramp: with nothing else above 4, stopping at 4 resources is right, and you go to 5 only for Ty Yorrick or for a turn with a 3-drop and a 2-drop.

- **1-drops:** 10 units (the 11th is Dorsal Turret, which can't be played without a Vehicle in play).
- **2-drops:** 25 cards, including Verdant Fortress, Reckless Sacrifice (2 copies), Nightfall (3 copies), Cloud-Rider (3), Y-Wing (3), Adamant Ewoks (3), Logray (3), and the 2-cost units. Verdant Fortress is the only upgrade at 2, so it competes for the same slot as the units.
- **3-drops:** V-19 (3), Ewok Archers (3), Heavy Ion Cannon (2 copies) and Turning the Tide (2). The Cannon is the only 3-drop that doesn't affect the board when it is played, but it replaces itself with a card draw, and every later use of it removes or weakens a unit.
- **4-drops:** Relentless Hunters (2 copies) and Han Solo (1).
- **5-drop:** Ty Yorrick (1).

At 4 resources you can cast a 2-drop and two 1-drops, or a 3-drop and a 1-drop, every round. A fifth resource is worth it for Ty Yorrick, for a turn with Relentless Hunters or Han Solo, or for a 3-drop and 2-drop in the same round.

### 3. Leader and base

- **Leader side:**
  - **Trigger:** Wicket draws whenever a cheaper unit attacks a more expensive one. That's once per round, because you exhaust him to do it.
  - **Who qualifies:** a 1-drop qualifies against anything costing 2 or more. A 2-drop qualifies against anything costing 3 or more, such as a Y-Wing carrying Dorsal Turret attacking a 3-cost ship.
  - **Pumps don't change cost:** Nightfall raises power, not printed cost.
  - **The Cannon doesn't trigger it:** the Cannon's damage isn't an attack.
- **Deploy (4 resources, turn 3):**
  - **Draw condition:** the deployed 2/5 draws on every attack while you control a unit costing 3 or less. 35 of the 39 units qualify, so it is almost always live. The four that don't are the two Relentless Hunters, Han Solo (4) and Ty Yorrick (5).
  - **Ready on deploy:** he deploys ready, even from exhausted (3.4.4, p. 15). So you can use the leader-side draw first, then deploy and attack for a second draw.
  - **Toughness:** 5 HP survives most pings. It doesn't survive two -3/-3 effects (Anakin Skywalker, Champion of Mortis), a 5-power attacker after a ping, or Topple the Summit after a ping.
- **Base:**
  - **What it does:** 30 HP and no ability of its own. The Endor trait powers 9 cards.
  - **Healing:** Leia and Village Tender are the only healing.
  - **Heavy Ion Cannon:** Fortify attaches it to your base (7.5.21, pp. 34–35), so the base becomes a source of damage that survives Wicket's defeat and every sweeper.
  - **Verdant Fortress:** also a Fortify upgrade on the base. It survives unit removal, but upgrade removal (for example Outer Rim Constable) can remove it, and it can't be attached to a unit or an enemy base.
  - **Fortify slots:** the Cannon and Verdant Fortress are both Fortify upgrades on the same base. Only an ability that defeats upgrades removes them. Whether two Fortify upgrades can sit on one base at once has not been checked in play, so confirm it before relying on both.

### 4. Weaknesses

- **Big ships:**
  - **Removal:** only Dorsal Turret kills a ship with more than 5 HP. That includes a Luke-piloted fighter (7/9), a Boba-piloted Justifier (8/8) or TIE Bomber (4/8), Chimaera (6/6) and Nightbrother (6/7). Reckless Sacrifice deals 6 with Ty Yorrick in play, which still doesn't kill an 8-HP ship. The main deck has only 1 Turret, and it needs a Vehicle that has survived a round. Two more are in the sideboard.
  - **Blocking:** Outland Protector and V-19 buy time. They don't kill anything.
  - **Single Turret:** with one copy in the main, the first Turret in hand depends on the deck. Expect it to be stranded (no ready Vehicle) more often than with two.
- **Big ground units:**
  - **Dorsal Turret is blank:** its carriers are space units and can't attack a ground unit.
  - **Removal:** Reckless Sacrifice deals 5 (6 with Ty Yorrick), so it kills a 5-HP unit (6-HP with Ty) but not a 7-HP one such as Captain Rex (7/7) or Hunter (4/7). Relentless Hunters attacks for 5. Han Solo is the one card that reaches 6–7: his three Advantage tokens on a ready Relentless Hunters make an 8-power Saboteur attack. But there is one copy in the main and one in the sideboard, and the tokens need a ready attacker that round. Turning the Tide needs five or more friendly units to deal 5.
  - **Your attacks bounce off:** your attackers have 1–3 power (4–5 with Verdant Fortress's Raid when attacking), and a 5–7 HP body survives two or three of them. Each unit you leave alive kills one of yours on its next attack.
- **Sweepers that scale:**
  - **Pings:** the 4- and 5-HP units shrug off pings.
  - **Real board wipes still kill everything:** Pre Vizsla defeats up to 6 total HP, and The Legacy Run deals 6 divided.
  - **Topple the Summit:** it deals 3 to each damaged unit, so any unit that has taken a ping dies to it.
  - **Small units die to anything:** Troublemaker (2), Adamant Ewoks (2), Benthic (2) and the Wicket unit (3).
  - **Refill:** Wicket's draw and the Cannon's When Played draw are the only refill.
- **Almost no ground Sentinel:** you can only race on the ground past Sentinel walls. The Saboteurs (Troublemaker, Wicket, Hunters, Han Solo) are the answer.
- **Little Hidden:** only Village Troublemaker and Village Tender have it, and nothing gives it to other units. Deployed Wicket and your 4–5 HP Ewoks can be attacked the turn they land.
- **No late game on an even board:** see the game-stage plan. Ty Yorrick is the only card that is still useful after turn 7 and doesn't rely on the board being wide.
- **Blank cards against decks with no ground units:**
  - **Need enemy ground units:** the Wicket unit (it can't attack bases), Benthic's ping and Archers' Ambush.
  - **Need the opponent to damage your units:** Logray.
  - **Needs expensive enemy units:** Wicket's leader-side draw.
  - **Needs enemy Creature units:** Ty Yorrick's On Attack. His static +1 still works without one.
- **Shields:**
  - **Damage cards:** Turning the Tide, Reckless Sacrifice and each Cannon shot are one instance of damage. A Shield soaks all of it.
  - **Dorsal Turret:** its defeat needs combat damage to land, so a Shield stops that too.
  - **Answers:** the Saboteurs defeat the Shield first, and Kaydel Connix and Desert Sharpshooter come in from the sideboard.
- **Upgrade removal:** Outer Rim Constable and similar cards defeat Heavy Ion Cannon or Verdant Fortress. The Cannon is still worth 3 resources if it fired once and drew a card.

### 5. Matchups

- **Fast aggro: even.**
  - **Why it's even:** the 4–5 HP units trade well with 2- and 3-drops. Logray punishes decks that attack your units, Leia heals, and the Cannon kills their 2-HP attackers.
  - **Racing:** you lose a pure race to anything faster.
- **Midrange (Tarfful, Sentinel walls): even.** These games go long and grind your units down.
  - **Get past Sentinels:** Cloud-Rider's base damage, plus Saboteur on Troublemaker, Hunters and Han Solo.
  - **Wicket:** hold him on the leader side and attack up each round for the draw.
  - **Clock:** their cards outclass yours after turn 8. Ty Yorrick and Nightfall are your late finishers.
- **Ping / sweeper control (Maul, Old Master): unfavourable.**
  - **How they win:** Maul plays a unit for 1 less and defeats it right away, so The Legacy Run's 6 divided damage lands for 4 resources. Chimaera, Anakin, Pre Vizsla and a Maul that replays units follow from turn 5.
  - **What carries it:** the 4–5 HP units mean each sweep kills fewer of yours. Saboteurs get past Imperial Armored Commando and Jod Na Nawood. Adamant Ewoks, Nightfall, Y-Wing and the Cannon deal damage that a sweep can't undo, and Ty Yorrick raises all of it by 1. Saw Gerrera (6 HP) comes in from the sideboard.
- **Indirect damage / ping (Boba Fett): unfavourable.**
  - **How they win:** they ping every unit with Mortar Trooper, IG-2000 and Justifier, then Topple the Summit kills every damaged unit. Boba deploys as a pilot at 6 resources onto a ship you can't kill. Planetary Bombardment and General Grievous finish the base.
  - **What carries it:** Dorsal Turret for Boba's ship (bring in the sideboard copies), the Cannon for their Droid Missile Platforms and Constables, and keeping your units undamaged when Topple is coming.
- **Slow ramp (few early plays, big ships): favourable.**
  - **Why:** you get several free rounds of base damage before their threats land.
  - **Removal:** Reckless Sacrifice (discard a 1-drop) deals 5 (6 with Ty) to their first big threat. Dorsal Turret kills a big ship. Turning the Tide or a Nightfall attack handles the next one. The Cannon finishes whatever is left damaged.
  - **Draws:** cheap units attacking up draw cards.
- **Ground midrange with big units (Chewbacca): unfavourable.**
  - **How they win:** from turn 2 nearly every unit they play has 4–7 HP. Many fight when they are played (Latts Razzi, Blue Leader's Ambush, Hunter, Captain Rex). Chewbacca's leader action then lets one unit attack again every round. You lose a unit every round without them losing a card.
  - **What carries it:** Adamant Ewoks, Reckless Sacrifice and the Cannon (damage that lands when played), Ty Yorrick's +1 on those three, Han Solo's +3 to kill a 6–7 HP unit, Nightfall used to finish a unit, the Saboteurs against Hunter's Shields, Desert Sharpshooter on an upgraded unit, and your space units hitting their base unopposed.
  - **What doesn't:** Dorsal Turret (they have no big ships) and Turning the Tide (your board is never wide enough).
- **Space / pilots (Luke Skywalker, Wedge Antilles): slightly unfavourable.**
  - **Blocking:** Outland Protector blocks a 7-power pilot ship for 1 resource, and V-19 blocks as well.
  - **Removal:** Dorsal Turret kills the ship. Turning the Tide kills an unpiloted 4-HP fighter. The Cannon kills 2-HP fighters and finishes damaged ones (3-HP fighters with Ty). Shoot Down (sideboard) kills a space unit outright.
  - **Air Superiority:** it only works if they control more space units than you, so play your ships early.
- **Leader-combo / Force: even.** You can kill a deployed leader several ways; otherwise race it:
  - Reckless Sacrifice, discarding a 1-drop;
  - Hunters' Saboteur;
  - Turning the Tide;
  - a Dorsal Turret ship, against space leaders;
  - a Nightfall attack.

## How to pilot it

### Mulligan

- **Keep** any six with two cheap units that can damage a base or hold a board. Village Tender, Logray and the Wicket unit don't count toward the two.
- **A mulligan is all-or-nothing.** It replaces the whole hand, and you must keep the new six (p. 22).

### Resources

- **Setup:** you see the opponent's leader and base before you choose your two setup resources. Use that.
  - **Against Luke, Wedge, Boba or Maul:** never resource Reckless Sacrifice, Dorsal Turret, Outland Protector or Heavy Ion Cannon at setup.
  - **Against a space deck:** don't resource your Y-Wings either.
  - **Ty Yorrick:** don't resource him at setup. He is your turn-4 five-drop.
  - **Space cards in hand:** resourcing a blocker or an Air-Superiority-proof ship at setup leaves the deck short on blockers once a big ship lands.
- **How many:** stop at 4. Go to 5 if you hold Ty Yorrick, or Relentless Hunters or Han Solo and need a 3-drop and a 2-drop in the same round. More resources beyond 5 just empty your hand faster.
- **Spare cards with a Cannon out:** once you have 4–5 resources and a Cannon in play, a spare card is usually worth more as Cannon fuel (2 damage, or 3 with Ty) than as a resource.
- **What to resource first:**
  1. Spare copies of uniques you already have in play.
  2. Village Tender.
  3. Dorsal Turret, against decks with no big ships.
  4. Outland Protector, against decks with no space units. Keep V-19 and Y-Wing, which become base attackers nobody can block.
  5. The Wicket unit, Logray and Ewok Archers, against decks with no ground units.
  6. Verdant Fortress, if the opponent has no units that attack your base often and you'd rather have a card's worth of resources.
- **Never resource your last Nightfall.** It is a finisher.
- **Never resource your last Reckless Sacrifice against a deck with big units.** It is one of the few answers to a 6+ HP threat.

### Turn by turn

- **Turn 1:** two 1-drops if you have them.
  - **Against a space deck:** play Outland Protector or Y-Wing, so your ships are already out when theirs arrive.
  - **No 1-drops:** play Cloud-Rider Veteran or Y-Wing, which start chipping on turn 2, or Adamant Ewoks (1 to the base).
  - **Leia:** play her before the other 1-drop only if your base is already damaged.
  - **No attacks:** units enter play exhausted, so nobody can attack on turn 1.
- **Turn 2:** a 2-drop plus a 1-drop.
  - **Verdant Fortress:** a good turn-2 play once you have two or more units out, because it adds Raid 1 to every attacker from then on. Play it before a turn when several units will attack.
  - **Default body:** Ewok Brigade against decks that ping. Champions of Endor otherwise.
  - **Chirpa:** best once two or three Ewoks are out. He is a 1/5, so he is also a good body against ping decks once he hits for 3 or more.
  - **Against space decks:** V-19 and Archers are 3-drops. Play V-19 early, so it is already guarding when their ships attack.
- **Turn 3:** attack a more expensive unit with a small one and exhaust Wicket to draw. Then deploy him and attack with him for a second draw. Play Heavy Ion Cannon on turn 3 only if you have no better unit to play.
- **Turn 4:** with 5 resources, play Ty Yorrick if you hold him. Otherwise play your 4-drop or a 3-drop and 1-drop.
- **Turns 4–6:**
  - **Play your hand:** play most of your hand and attack with everything. Cloud-Rider and Y-Wing attack every round.
  - **The Cannon:** a Cannon plus a 1-drop is a good 4-resource turn.
  - **Against a sweeper deck:** keep two units in hand (see "Playing around sweepers").
- **Turn 7+:** count damage. Close with Nightfall on Cloud-Rider, Y-Wing, Hunters or a Saboteur, and use the Cannon each round on whatever blocks.

### When not to deploy Wicket on turn 3

- **Deploy at once** if the opponent has no units, or only 1-cost ground units (pilot decks). The leader side draws nothing then.
- **Hold him** if their board would kill a 2/5 for free and they keep playing expensive units, because the leader side keeps drawing safely. Do this against midrange.
- **Against Maul or Boba,** he can't dodge Anakin's -3/-3 (twice), The Legacy Run, Fennec Shand or Topple the Summit. Deploy him when he can attack straight away, so he draws at least once.
- **He has no protection the turn he lands.** Nothing in the deck gives him Hidden, so any ready enemy unit can attack him right after he deploys.
- **If he is defeated,** he flips back exhausted and can't redeploy (3.4.5, p. 16). His leader-side draw works again from the next round.

### Card-specific lines

- **Hidden** only stops attacks, and only in the phase the unit was played. It doesn't stop damage from abilities or events, which is how the ping decks kill Ewoks.
- **Village Troublemaker:** with your Endor base it always has Hidden and Saboteur. It is the cheapest way past a Sentinel, and a strong Nightfall target.
- **Wicket the unit:** it can't attack bases. Use it to snipe behind Sentinels (Saboteur) and to attack up for draws.
- **Leia:** she heals when you play another unit costing 3 or less. She does nothing while your base is undamaged, or after General Grievous (Scourge of Dathomir) is in play, because then bases can't be healed.
- **Verdant Fortress:**
  - **What it does:** every friendly unit gains Raid 1 while attacking. That is +1/+0 for the attack, and it applies to the Wicket unit, Cloud-Rider, Relentless Hunters, Ty Yorrick and every Vehicle when it attacks.
  - **Wide boards:** the more units you attack with, the more total power it adds. Four attackers get +4.
  - **Ends with the attack:** the Raid bonus is lost when the attack ends (p. 32). It does nothing on defense.
  - **Fortify rules:** attaches only to your base, and ignores unit-based play restrictions (7.5.21, pp. 34–35). It can be played onto an empty board.
- **Ty Yorrick, Monster Hunter:**
  - **What it does:** "If a friendly ability would deal damage, you may have that ability deal that much damage plus 1 instead." It is a replacement effect (7.7, p. 38), and the rules' own example uses this card: a 4-damage ability becomes 5, not an unlimited amount.
  - **Your damage that gets +1:** Logray's ping (1 → 2), Adamant Ewoks' base damage and unit damage (1 → 2), Nightfall's ping (1 → 2), Benthic's On Attack and When Defeated (1 → 2), Cloud-Rider's base damage (2 → 3), Y-Wing's indirect damage (3 → 4), the Cannon (2 → 3), Reckless Sacrifice (5 → 6), and Turning the Tide (one extra point to the total, which is the reading to confirm in play).
  - **Combat damage doesn't count:** it is not an ability, so Ty adds nothing to attacks, and Verdant Fortress's Raid is the only bonus for attacking.
  - **Your own cards:** Han Solo's 3 self-damage is a friendly ability too, so Ty would let you make it 4. Decline it. Pick the number you want each time, because the card says "may".
  - **Creature units:** Ty's On Attack (1 damage to an enemy Creature unit) only does something against a Creature. The +1 works without one.
  - **Toughness:** 5 HP. He survives one ping plus a Topple the Summit (1 + 3 = 4 damage), but two pings and a Topple kill him.
  - **Cost:** 5 resources means turn 4 with a resource every turn. That turn he takes your whole budget, so you cast nothing else.
- **Outland Protector:**
  - **What it does:** a 1-cost space Sentinel (7.5.11, p. 32). Its job is to soak one hit from a pilot ship or a big capital ship.
  - **Why the block holds:** the enemy ship's On Attack ability can kill it before combat damage, and the attack still deals nothing, because the defender has left play (6.3 step 4.c, p. 26). The only exception is an attacker with Overwhelm.
  - **When to play it:** against ping decks, hold it until there is a big ship to block. A Protector played early against a ping deck dies to pings before any big ship arrives.
  - **Other uses:** it is a Vehicle, so it can carry a Dorsal Turret. It protects your Y-Wings, counts toward Turning the Tide and Archers' Ambush, and keeps Wicket's deployed draw live.
- **Dorsal Turret:**
  - **Targets:** put it on a ready Y-Wing, V-19 or Protector and attack the biggest enemy ship. Any combat damage defeats it: a Luke- or Boba-piloted ship, Chimaera, Nightbrother.
  - **HP doesn't matter:** the defeat is not damage. Your ship usually dies too, which is a good trade.
  - **Leaders:** a ship carrying a leader pilot is a leader unit, and the Turret still defeats it. That makes it your only clean answer to Luke or Boba as a pilot.
  - **Shields:** a Shield prevents the combat damage, so the defeat never triggers. Remove the Shield first.
  - **The Legacy Run:** don't use it on The Legacy Run while you have units in play, because its When Defeated deals 6 damage divided among your units.
  - **Timing:** a ship played this round is exhausted. Keep the Turret in hand until a ship has survived a round, then play it and attack in the same action. A Nightfall attack with a Turret ship also works.
  - **No Vehicle:** an upgrade with no eligible unit goes to the discard pile (6.2 step 5.b, p. 25), so don't play it without one. A stranded Turret is good Cannon fuel.
- **Heavy Ion Cannon:**
  - **What it is:** a Fortify upgrade. It attaches to your base, not a unit (7.5.21, pp. 34–35), and draws a card when played.
  - **The action:** once each phase, discard a card to deal 2 damage to any unit in either arena (3 with Ty Yorrick in play).
  - **Two copies:** each copy gives the base its own copy of the ability. On a plain reading, two Cannons give two shots per phase, but the rules don't cover this case directly, so check the first time it comes up.
  - **What it kills alone:** units with 2 HP or less, such as Droid Missile Platform, Outer Rim Constable, and other 2-HP units in either arena. With Ty in play, also 3-HP units.
  - **What it finishes:** after one ping or attack, it finishes 3-HP units such as Fennec Shand, Third Sister, Zygerrian Starhopper and Jod Na Nawood. Against a big ship, it adds 2 (3 with Ty) to a ship your attacks have already damaged.
  - **What to discard,** best first:
    1. A spare copy of a unique already in play.
    2. A Dorsal Turret with no Vehicle to carry it.
    3. An Outland Protector against a deck with no big ships.
    4. Village Tender, or a 1-drop late in the game.
  - **What not to discard:** your last Nightfall, or your last Reckless Sacrifice against big units. Reckless Sacrifice also needs a spare unit to discard, so keep one for it if you hold it.
  - **Timing:**
    - **Before your attacks:** shoot a blocker or a 2-HP Sentinel to open the way.
    - **After your attacks:** finish a unit they damaged.
    - **Your own units:** don't shoot your own units to trigger Logray. A card for a 1-damage ping is a bad trade.
  - **Cost of use:** each shot is an action and a card. With a 2–4 card hand, expect to fire it about once a round.
- **Adamant Ewoks:** the Endor base means the ping always works, even on an empty board. Its damage lands when it is played and can't be answered first.
- **Logray:**
  - **The trigger:** every time one of your cheap units takes damage, you ping. That includes damage from The Legacy Run, Luke's ping, Red Five and Mortar Trooper, so the more of your units survive, the more you ping.
  - **When he is strong:** against decks that damage your units. He is blank against decks that only attack your base.
  - **With Ty Yorrick:** his ping is 2 damage, which finishes a 2-HP unit in one trigger.
  - **Against Topple the Summit:** don't put indirect damage on your own units to feed him, because every damaged unit dies to Topple.
- **Ewok Brigade:** a plain 2/4 Ewok. It survives Luke's ping plus Red Five's 2, and on a 4-HP board The Legacy Run's 6 divided damage kills at most one unit. Against decks that don't ping, Champions of Endor (3/3) hits harder.
- **Ewok Archers:**
  - **Ambush:** with another unit costing 3 or less out, it can attack an enemy unit the turn it is played (7.5.5, p. 31). It is still exhausted afterwards, and it can't Ambush a base. Ty Yorrick (5) doesn't count toward this condition, but any other unit costing 3 or less does.
  - **Best use:** kill 2-HP ground units, or finish a damaged one.
  - **Toughness:** at 5 HP it survives every ping and The Legacy Run's split.
- **Chief Chirpa:** +1 power per other Ewok unit, including deployed Wicket. Leia, Benthic, Cloud-Rider, Y-Wing, V-19, Protector, Hunters, Han Solo and Ty Yorrick don't count. There are 20 Ewok units in the deck, so he usually hits for 3–5 by turn 4, and his 5 HP survives every ping and The Legacy Run's split. Play him once two or three Ewoks are out. He is unique: if you draw the second copy while the first is in play, resource it or use it as Cannon or Reckless Sacrifice fuel. Playing it would force you to defeat one of the two (1.16.b, p. 11), which only makes sense if the one in play is about to die anyway.
- **Benthic:** the attack ping hits ground units only. His 1 base damage on defeat makes him a fine unit to trade away.
- **Champions of Endor:** a plain 3/3 Ewok. It trades with most 2- and 3-drops.
- **Cloud-Rider Veteran:**
  - **Base damage:** the 2 base damage happens whatever it attacks, so a Sentinel doesn't stop it (3 with Ty Yorrick).
  - **Attacking up:** attacking a unit that costs 3 or more gets the base damage and Wicket's leader-side draw.
  - **Weak spots:** ping decks target it first. Its 4 HP survives one ping, but not Air Superiority (4 damage) or a ping followed by Topple the Summit.
- **Red Squadron Y-Wing:**
  - **Indirect damage:** the opponent splits the 3 indirect damage between their base and units, and it can't be prevented (8.35, p. 47). With Ty Yorrick it is 4.
  - **What to attack:** attack the base unless a space unit must die. Attacking the base deals up to 4 a turn (1 combat plus 3 indirect). Expect less: opponents often put the damage on units, and a 1-cost space Sentinel or an Ambush ship stops it.
  - **Turret carrier:** it is the cheapest Dorsal Turret carrier.
- **V-19 Skirmisher:**
  - **Sentinel:** it counts itself toward the three units, so it becomes a Sentinel once two other units are out (7.5.11, p. 32). Count your units before relying on it: with only two units in play, it is not a Sentinel, and an enemy ship can attack your base past it.
  - **Against decks with no space units:** it is a 3-power base attacker that can't be blocked on the ground.
  - **Toughness:** at 3 HP it dies to a pilot ship's On Attack, so it buys one turn at most.
- **Reckless Sacrifice:**
  - **Targeting:** the target must cost more than the unit you discard. A discarded 1-drop hits anything costing 2 or more. Discarding Hunters only hits units costing 5 or more. It never hits a 1-cost unit, so it can't touch 1-cost ships carrying pilots.
  - **Damage:** 5, or 6 with Ty Yorrick in play. That kills 5–6 HP units cleanly, such as a deployed Chewbacca (3/6).
  - **Reach:** it can hit space units and deployed leaders.
  - **Fuel:** keep one spare cheap unit in hand while you hold it. A spare unique 1-drop is the best discard.
  - **Stolen AT-Hauler:** if you defeat it, you may play it from their discard pile for free that phase, which would be a free 4/5 for you. Not yet confirmed in play.
- **Turning the Tide:**
  - **Damage:** it counts friendly units in both arenas, so it deals 4–6 by turns 4–5.
  - **Targets:** save it for a unit you can't remove in combat, such as an unpiloted ship, a Sentinel or a deployed leader.
  - **Shields:** it is one instance of damage, so a Shield soaks all of it.
- **Relentless Hunters:** the Saboteur that can attack bases. Play it the first round you can, or resource it. Holding it for later wastes it.
- **Han Solo, It'll Work:**
  - **Advantage tokens:** each gives +1/+0, and each is defeated when the attached unit's attack or defense ends (3.7.15, p. 18). So the +3 lasts for one attack, or until the unit is attacked.
  - **Best use:** play Han, put all three tokens on a ready unit that hasn't attacked this round, then attack with it. Relentless Hunters becomes an 8-power Saboteur, Chirpa with three other Ewoks becomes 7, deployed Wicket becomes 5. That kills a 6–7 HP ground unit such as Hunter, Captain Rex or deployed Chewbacca, and Saboteur strips a Shield first.
  - **Hitting the base:** the same play on Cloud-Rider (4 power) or Y-Wing (4 power) adds 3 to the base along with their On Attack damage. Do this when the opponent's base is in range.
  - **No ready unit:** the tokens can go on Han himself for 6 power next round, but they are lost if he is attacked first. Do this only when nothing else is ready.
  - **The 3 self-damage:** Han ends at 4 HP left. Logray doesn't trigger, because Han costs 4. Against Boba, a damaged Han still survives Topple the Summit (3 + 3 = 6 damage on 7 HP).
  - **Never give the tokens to an enemy unit.** "A unit" includes theirs.
  - **Wicket's leader-side draw:** Han costs 4, so attacking with him only draws against units costing 5 or more.
- **Ty Yorrick as an attacker:** he costs 5, so attacking with him draws off Wicket's leader side only against a unit costing 6 or more. His 4 power (5 with Han's tokens) is strong on the ground.
- **Nightfall:** you need a ready unit for the attack half. The best targets are:
  - Cloud-Rider (5 to the base in total, 6 with Ty's +1 on the base damage);
  - Hunters or Troublemaker past a Sentinel;
  - Y-Wing for a second 3 indirect;
  - a Dorsal Turret ship into a big ship.
- **Nightfall against a deck whose units attack again (Chewbacca):** use the 1 damage and the +2 attack together to kill a damaged unit, not to hit the base. A unit left at 2 HP after the ping dies to any attack from a unit with 2 or more power after the +2.
- **Desert Sharpshooter (sideboard):** 2 damage to an upgraded ground unit when played. It answers Shield tokens and upgrade-heavy decks, but the upgrade has to be on a ground unit.
- **Saw Gerrera (sideboard):** a 4-cost 3/6 Command/Heroism. It survives two pings and a Legacy Run split, and each time an opponent plays an event you resource the top card of your deck.

### Initiative

Taking the initiative ends your actions for the round (p. 9), so take it only when you have nothing better left. It is worth more here than in most decks. Units played this round stop being Hidden next round, and acting first lets them attack before they can be attacked.

An opponent who claims it every round will remove your best attacker with their first action each round. Against that, lean on damage that lands when a card is played (Adamant Ewoks, Nightfall, the Cannon, Reckless Sacrifice), and attack first with the unit most likely to be removed.

### Playing around sweepers

- **Against a deck with The Legacy Run, Pre Vizsla or a similar sweeper:** keep three or four units out, mostly 4–5 HP, and hold two units in hand.
- **What the held units do:** they rebuild the board after the sweep, and they are fuel for Reckless Sacrifice and the Cannon.
- **What keeps pressure on:** Cloud-Rider, Y-Wing and the Cannon keep the clock going on their own.
- **Against Topple the Summit:** the danger is damage already on your units, not the number of units. Keep your units undamaged whenever a Topple is likely, and don't feed Logray indirect damage before it lands.

### Playing around bounce and exhaust

Against decks that return or exhaust your units (Razor Crest, Death Space Skirmisher, Jod Na Nawood), attack with your biggest ready unit before you play cards. A unit that has already attacked loses nothing that round if it is bounced or exhausted.
